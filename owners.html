The Upstate Opportunity

What Most Upstate Owners Are Quietly Settling For

Greenville & Travelers Rest are among the strongest short-term rental markets in the Southeast. Most homes here are managed like it's still 2016.

Pricing gets set once and left alone. Listing photos never get refreshed. Guests are treated like transactions. And plenty of owners — especially second-home and out-of-town owners — have no idea whether anyone has actually walked through their property since the last guest left.

Meanwhile the market keeps rewarding the homes that are marketed well, priced daily, stocked to a five-star standard, and genuinely cared for. That gap between what a home could earn and what it does earn is the whole ballgame — and it's exactly where we live.

"A great home in a great market still underperforms when it's marketed like an afterthought. Most of the upside is in the details nobody's tending to."

The fee math worth running

If your Greenville home earns $110,000 a year and a manager takes 25–30%, you're writing a check for $27,500–$33,000 every year.

We price meaningfully below that — and, more importantly, we tend to grow the top line through better marketing, daily pricing, and higher-converting listings. We don't publish a flat rate because we price to your property. But we'll always tell you the number before you sign.

02

We market to our own database of past guests

Every stay grows a list of proven, happy travelers. We market your home directly to that database by email — driving repeat visits and off-platform direct bookings you'd never get by waiting on the OTAs alone.

03

AI-driven daily pricing

Rates are updated every single day against real demand — events, weekends, festivals, shoulder season — not a flat number set in January. That's occupancy in the slow months and top dollar when Greenville is packed.

04

Keyless, five-star-standard homes

100% keyless entry — no lockboxes, no key handoffs. Professional photography and listing copy that converts, plus homes stocked to the standard that earns five-star reviews. Your reviews are your pricing power.

05

Vetted local cleaners, a trusted maintenance network, and same-day response because we're already here. Post-stay inspections with photos after every turnover — so small issues get caught before they become expensive ones.

06

Complete transparency — no surprises

You know what you'll earn before you sign. Monthly statements you can actually read, with no mystery line items, and reimbursements handled cleanly. We think like asset owners, not booking agents.
