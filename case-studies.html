Greenville, SC · Saluda River

Saluda River Basecamp

A basic riverfront house, reimagined as a top-performing luxury retreat.

Great bones, an unbeatable spot on the Saluda River — and not much else. Basecamp led a full transformation: professionally designed interiors, themed bedrooms, and a resort-grade amenity stack built for how people actually vacation in the Upstate. An eight-person hot tub over the water, a four-person barrel sauna, a cold plunge, private river access, and a dedicated game room turned a plain rental into a destination.

Pre-purchase underwriting projected roughly $85K in gross revenue. The design-led approach blew past that — and it's still climbing.

Downtown Greenville · 3-Unit Retreat

Downtown 3-Unit Retreat

Self-managed and under-earning — until the strategy changed.

This three-unit property sits within walking distance of downtown Greenville, but it was being self-managed and leaving money on the table. We added targeted, high-impact amenities to each unit, rebuilt pricing around live demand instead of flat nightly rates, and launched a dedicated marketing plan across Airbnb, VRBO, and direct booking.

The combination tripled revenue. And the number that matters most to an owner: even after our management commission, their take-home was 30% higher than what they'd cleared self-managing.
