On the Water

The Saluda River & Swamp Rabbit Trail

Launch a kayak straight from Saluda River Basecamp, or tube the gentle stretches with the Saluda Outdoor River Company nearby. Then ride the Swamp Rabbit — 28 paved miles linking downtown Greenville to Travelers Rest along the river.

  • Kayaking & tubing on the Saluda
  • 28-mile Swamp Rabbit Trail
  • Riverside dining at the TR trailheads
Into the Mountains

Blue Wall, Waterfalls & State Parks

The Blue Ridge Escarpment starts just north of Travelers Rest. Hike the Blue Wall Preserve, chase waterfalls around Caesars Head, and summit the classic trails at Table Rock and Paris Mountain.

  • Paris Mountain State Park — 20 min
  • Caesars Head & Table Rock — big views
  • Raven Cliff Falls & the US-276 corridor

★ Featured Day Trip

Lake Robinson & Cole's Kayak Rental

For a laid-back day on the water with real mountain views, head up to Lake Robinson near Greer. Grab a kayak or fishing boat from Cole's Bait Shop & Kayak Rental — they'll even deliver and pick up — then spend the afternoon paddling a quiet, scenic lake tucked into the Blue Ridge foothills. Easy, fun, and perfect for families.
